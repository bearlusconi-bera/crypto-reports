## 19. Product Implications

**Roadmap impact:**
- **Morgan Stanley ETH staking ETP sets the institutional bar:** 0.14% expense ratio + 95% reward passthrough means any competing ETH staking product needs to match or beat this. Fee compression will accelerate across all staking wrappers.
- **Robinhood Chain is the benchmark for tokenized equities distribution:** 200+ stocks, $10B Uniswap volume in <1 month, 100M transactions. Any competing tokenized stock platform must answer "how do you get to 27M users?"
- **Clarity Act + GENIUS Act = complete US crypto regulatory framework:** If Clarity passes, the combination gives banks statutory authority (staking, custody, lending) + stablecoin issuance rules. Product teams should design for this regulatory end-state, not the current ambiguity.
- **Fullstack AI benchmarks change model selection:** Arena's fullstack rankings (Kimi K3 > GPT 5.6 Sol > Claude Fable 5) matter more than text-only benchmarks for shipping products. Route model selection by task type, not brand.

- Fanatics acquiring a CFTC exchange brings 100M+ sports fans to prediction markets — a bigger distribution play than Polymarket or Kalshi.
- Venice obtaining its own ASN is vertical integration from the network layer — deep infrastructure commitment that positions it as the anti-OpenAI.
