## 17. Product Implications

**Roadmap Impact:**
- **The BTC stagnation narrative is now the dominant frame.** With ETF + corporate treasury demand collapsed, the market needs a new catalyst. Product teams building BTC-denominated products (vaults, yield, structured products) should model for sustained $60K-$65K range rather than assuming a return to 2025's upward trajectory.

Any product that assumes USDC/USDT market-share dominance should model for bank-issued stablecoin competition within 12 months.
- **The AI agent economy has concrete pricing.** Circle's $0.15-$1.08 USDC agent workflows + Stripe/Tempo's Machine Payments Protocol + Matt Huang's "thesis validated" = the agent-payment rail is production-ready. Products that can accept/generate USDC micropayments for AI agent workflows have a new demand vector.

Product implication: issue on Ethereum for credibility, but route trading to Solana. Multi-chain strategy is no longer optional for tokenized-asset products.
