- What happened: A coalition of Bitcoin developers (Jameson Lopp, Christian Papathanasiou, Ian Smith, Joe Ross, Steve Vaile, Pierre-Luc Dallaire-Demers) introduced BIP-361, a three-phase plan to freeze bitcoins in quantum-vulnerable wallet formats. ## 15. Product Implications

**Roadmap Impact:**
- **Distribution channel security is now the #1 attack vector.** Two incidents in 48 hours — CoW Swap DNS hijack (yesterday, $500K) and fake Ledger App Store app ($9.5M). Smart contracts are hardening; app stores, DNS, and frontends are the soft underbelly. Every DeFi product needs a distribution security audit.
- **BIP-361 creates a 3-5 year migration deadline for Bitcoin products.** If adopted, any product handling legacy BTC addresses needs a quantum-safe migration path. Custodians, exchanges, and wallet providers should start planning now — even if the timeline is long, the governance battle starts immediately.
- **Non-USD stablecoins are a real product category now.** Brix's iTRY (40% Turkish lira yield) with Circle Ventures backing, plus HSBC getting a HK stablecoin license — the multi-currency stablecoin thesis is materializing. Products should design for multi-denomination stablecoin support.
- **AI is now both the attack tool and the defense tool in crypto security.** DL News reports exchanges racing for Claude access; Base launched Frosty yesterday. The gap between AI-equipped and AI-unequipped security teams will widen fast.

**Competitor Moves:**
- X Money with potential stablecoin features: Warren's letter confirms this is real and imminent. Could be the largest crypto-adjacent payments product by user base.
- HSBC as licensed stablecoin issuer: a Tier 1 global bank entering stablecoin issuance.
- ICE launching USDC rate futures: stablecoin yield benchmarks entering TradFi derivatives.
- Brix/MegaETH: non-USD yield-bearing stablecoins targeting emerging markets.

**What to Test:**
- App Store/distribution verification: review your wallet app's listing for potential impersonators.
- Legacy BTC address exposure: audit how much of your custody/product touches P2PK/P2PKH formats.
- Multi-denomination stablecoin UX: can your product handle iTRY, HSBC-issued HKD stables, etc.?

**What to Avoid:**
- Assuming Apple/Google app store review is a security guarantee — it clearly isn't.
- Ignoring BIP-361 because "quantum is far away" — the governance process starts now and will take years.
- Building prediction market products without state-by-state regulatory analysis (Ohio precedent).

**Open Questions:**
- Will BIP-361 gain enough community support to advance, or will the "freezing coins is censorship" argument kill it?
- Does the Drift class action create precedent for DeFi protocol governance liability?
- What does X Money's stablecoin integration look like, and will GENIUS Act carveouts apply?
- Will other states follow Ohio's Kalshi precedent?
