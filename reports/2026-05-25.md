- What happened: The Ethereum Foundation and major wallet developers launched "Clear Signing" (May 12), a new standard that replaces confusing transaction bytecode with human-readable explanations before users approve transactions.
- Why it matters: Addresses one of crypto's biggest UX failures — users blindly signing transactions they don't understand. If widely adopted, this could meaningfully reduce phishing and approval-based exploits.
- Who it impacts: Wallet providers, dapp frontends, security teams, end users.
