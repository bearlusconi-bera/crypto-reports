## 19. Product Implications

**Roadmap impact:**
- The OCC/FDIC reputational-risk rule removal is the most significant banking-regulatory shift for crypto since SAB 121. Every product that requires bank partnerships (fiat on/off ramps, stablecoin issuance, custody integration) should reassess its banking strategy — doors that were closed are now opening.
- Tokenized stock yield farming (phtevenstrong's Robinhood vs Base comparison) is creating a new product category: DeFi yield on equities. Products need to decide if they're a trading venue or a yield venue — the two are diverging.
- cbBTC on Robinhood Chain via Chainlink CCIP means BTC-collateral products (lending, borrowing, leverage) are now viable on the Robinhood ecosystem. First movers building BTC yield strategies on Robinhood Chain have a window.

**Competitor moves:**
- Charles Schwab adding SOL/AVAX/LINK to 40M accounts is a distribution event that pressures Coinbase and Fidelity to match. The brokerage-to-crypto pipeline is now a three-horse race (Schwab, Fidelity, Robinhood).
- Plume joining the DTCC working group alongside Schwab and Nasdaq positions it as the institutional RWA chain — a credibility moat that's hard to replicate.
- Midas mWIN on Aave Horizon (Wellington Management backing) sets the bar for what institutional collateral looks like in DeFi — $1.2T AUM asset managers backing tokens as lending collateral.

**What to test:**
- The Moonwell exploit supply-cap bypass pattern: audit every lending protocol fork on Base and other L2s for similar supply-cap enforcement vulnerabilities. This is likely not unique to Moonwell.
- Garry Tan's "AI generates more capital than it consumes" thesis vs. Bankless's "AI capital crunch" framing — which is true determines whether AI-linked crypto tokens (compute, inference, data) are investment-constrained or deployment-constrained.

**What to avoid:**
- Launching DeFi products on Base without thorough supply-cap and edge-case testing — the Moonwell exploit creates near-term reputation risk for the ecosystem.
- Ignoring the OCC/FDIC rule change: competitors who move faster on banking partnerships will capture distribution advantages that compound.

**Open questions:**
- Will Schwab's altcoin expansion trigger a brokerage war (Fidelity adding more tokens, Merrill entering)?
- How does the Moonwell exploit affect Base's institutional credibility, especially for Coinbase's tokenized stock products that launched yesterday?
- Does citrini's AI basket downweight signal a broader thematic rotation from AI to tokenized real assets — and if so, is Plume's DTCC positioning the play?
