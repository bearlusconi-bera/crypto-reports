- Robinhood is positioning tokenization as ownership-democratization (not crypto trading). This narrative is designed to survive any regulatory environment.
- Solana is emerging as the preferred chain for both tokenized equities and AI agent payments — two of the three fastest-growing crypto verticals.
- Circle's dominance in agentic payments (99.3% market share) is a near-monopoly. Challengers need a differentiated angle.
