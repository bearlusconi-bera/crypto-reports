## 1. Security Crisis — The Worst Month in Crypto History

April saw ~$900M+ stolen across 30 separate incidents — the most-hacked month on record.

**The Big Two:**
- **Drift Protocol (Apr 1): $285M** — Solana's largest DEX exploit. DPRK-linked attackers spent months posing as a quant firm, used Solana's durable nonces to trick Security Council members into pre-signing malicious transactions, then minted a fake token (CVT) as collateral to drain real assets. Fund tracked to Ethereum via Circle CCTP.
- **Kelp DAO (Apr 18-19): $292M** — 116,500 rsETH drained via LayerZero bridge exploit. Cascaded across Aave, SparkLend, Fluid. DeFi TVL dropped $6.4B in hours. Attacker laundered through Arbitrum and Tron.

**The Response — "DeFi United":**
- Unprecedented cross-protocol mutual aid: Ether.fi pledged 5,000 ETH, Stani Kulechov personally pledged 5,000 ETH, Lido contributed 2,500 stETH, Golem Foundation 1,000 ETH, Arbitrum froze ~$71M. Solana Foundation deployed treasury to lend USDT to Aave as cross-ecosystem solidarity.
- First time competing protocols coordinated a bail-out at this scale. Sets precedent for DeFi self-insurance.

**Other Attacks:**
- CoW Swap DNS hijack ($500K, Apr 14)
- Fake Ledger app on Apple App Store ($9.5M, 50+ victims, Apr 16)
- Bitwarden CLI supply chain attack (Apr 24)
- Vercel breach — crypto devs scrambled to rotate API keys (Apr 20)
- axios npm supply chain attack (Apr 1)

**The Bigger Picture:**
- DPRK operations confirmed behind multiple incidents — zachxbt exposed IT worker operations, SEAL published 164 IOCs
- Social engineering now primary vector — attacks shifting from smart contracts to humans (team members, signers, key holders)
- Jefferies warned banks may rethink blockchain plans due to the exploit wave
- Dan Robinson (Paradigm) and Haseeb (Dragonfly) publicly questioned whether DeFi lending is underpricing risk
