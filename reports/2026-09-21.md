## 19. Product Implications

**Roadmap Impact:**
- **BTC at $85K resets the recovery playbook.** If your product roadmap was calibrated to a $65K-$80K BTC range (as Polymarket implied yesterday), it needs recalibration. Higher BTC means higher TVL, higher trading volumes, higher fee revenue, and more aggressive user acquisition budgets across the ecosystem. Plan for both scenarios: sustained breakout above $85K vs. retracement to $80K.
- **iOS mobile security needs a rethink.** The FomoPeek exploit proves that Apple's App Store review is not a sufficient security layer for crypto wallet users. If your product is a mobile wallet or has mobile key management, consider: attestation checks for app integrity, server-side validation of client identity, and user-facing warnings about recently updated apps from unknown developers. The 8-day window (Sep 9-17) before removal is an eternity in crypto security.
- **Probabilistic product thinking for AI-DeFi.** Nikita Bier's "deterministic vs. probabilistic" frame is directly applicable: if you're building AI-augmented DeFi products (agent trading, AI risk assessment, LLM-powered interfaces), your UX must communicate uncertainty, not just output. Confidence intervals, probabilistic ranges, and explicit "the AI is X% sure" indicators are the interface primitives for AI-DeFi.
- **Token emissions ≠ revenue.** ImperiumPaper's analysis and borjaneira_'s stablecoin yield debate both point to the same product truth: if your growth metrics depend on token-subsidized incentives (emissions, cashback, yield), you need a clear path to organic demand before emissions taper.
