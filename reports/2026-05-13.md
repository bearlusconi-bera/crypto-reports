**4. Ethereum Foundation Unveils 'Clear Signing' Standard (ERC-7730) to End Blind Signing**
- What happened: The Ethereum Foundation launched a new "Clear Signing" initiative through the Trillion Dollar Security Initiative working group, which includes wallet developers and security firms. The standard (ERC-7730) replaces the confusing hex data users see when approving transactions with human-readable descriptions of what they're actually agreeing to. A public registry for verified transaction descriptions is part of the rollout.
- Why it matters: Blind signing has been one of crypto's most damaging UX failures — responsible for approval scams, phishing attacks, and accidental fund loss. If major wallets adopt ERC-7730, it removes one of the primary attack vectors that has drained hundreds of millions from users. This is the kind of infrastructure improvement that makes crypto safer at the protocol level rather than relying on user vigilance.
- Who it impacts: Wallet developers (MetaMask, Rabby, Rainbow), security firms, all Ethereum users, phishing/scam attackers (negatively).
- Link: https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2026/05/12/the-ethereum-foundation-unveils-new-clear-signing-standard-to-stop-users-from-approving-malicious-crypto-transactions
