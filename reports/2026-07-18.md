## 13. Regulation / Policy

**GENIUS Act rulemaking deadline (today)**
- Six federal agencies are finalizing GENIUS Act stablecoin rules by July 18, 2026. The law creates a federal framework for payment stablecoins, restricts issuance to regulated institutions, and classifies compliant stablecoins as neither securities nor commodities.
- **Product implication:** Any stablecoin-adjacent product needs to evaluate compliance with the new framework. OCC rulemaking will determine specific reserve, reporting, and licensing requirements.

**CLARITY Act advancing**
- The Senate Banking Committee advanced the CLARITY Act (15-9 vote, May 14) establishing market structure rules for tokenized assets. Anchorage [called](https://x.com/Anchorage/status/2078134199572398465) for "full market structure rules" as the next step beyond GENIUS.
- **Product implication:** CLARITY could define which tokenized assets are securities vs. commodities — critical for any tokenized product roadmap.
