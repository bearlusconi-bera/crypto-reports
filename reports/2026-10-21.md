## 3. Solana

**Alpenglow enters mainnet activation**
- What happened: Solana’s epoch 1064 activation window began today. Alpenglow replaces the TowerBFT voting system and targets finality around 150ms. Validators exchange votes off-chain; the target is not a guarantee of end-to-end confirmation time in every application.
- Why it matters: Faster finality could make payments, trading and bridge releases feel more responsive. Removing validator-vote transactions from blocks may also lower headline transaction counts without an equivalent fall in user activity.
- Who it impacts: Solana validators, wallets, exchanges, applications and teams that depend on finality signals.
- Link: [Solana Foundation upgrade notes](https://solana.com/upgrades/alpenglow), [CoinDesk launch coverage](https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2026/10/21/solana-s-alpenglow-its-biggest-network-overhaul-since-2020-goes-live)

## 12. Security / Incidents

**Qubic restarts after a weak-seed incident and opens claims**
- What happened: An attacker drained 1,404 wallets between Oct. 17 and 18, taking about 2.94 trillion QU, reported at roughly $1.3–1.4M. Qubic attributed the issue to insufficient randomness in wallet seed generation, rather than a flaw in its consensus or smart-contract protocol. The network resumed on Oct. 19; affected users can file claims through May 1, 2027.
- Why it matters: Updating the wallet fixes new seed generation, but it does not make an old, potentially exposed seed safe. The incident also shows how wallet implementation bugs can compromise funds without a chain-level exploit.
- Who it impacts: Qubic wallet users, exchanges and teams responsible for key generation and recovery.
- Link: [Cointelegraph incident update](https://cointelegraph.com/news/qubic-restarts-blockchain-opens-recovery-claims-after-14m-hack)
