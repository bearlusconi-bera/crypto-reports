## 15. Product Implications

**Roadmap Impact:**
- **DNS security is now a board-level DeFi risk** — CoW Swap's $500K loss from a frontend hijack (not a smart contract exploit) shows the attack surface has shifted. If you run a DeFi frontend, implement DNS monitoring, DNSSEC, and incident response playbooks today.
- **Tokenized assets are gaining real composability** — Morpho (S&P 500 as collateral) + Ondo (equities as perp margin) + Midas (looping vaults) = tokenized RWAs are no longer just "buy and hold." They're becoming DeFi primitives. Build for this composability.
- **Tether's consumer wallet play changes stablecoin distribution** — Gasless sends + human-readable addresses + $0.00001 fees. If Tether captures even a fraction of its existing USDT holders as wallet users, it becomes the largest self-custodial wallet overnight. Evaluate competitive positioning.
- **US stablecoin regulation is converging fast** — GENIUS Act + CLARITY Act + FDIC rules + Treasury AML all moving simultaneously. Products touching stablecoins need a regulatory monitoring function.

**Competitor Moves:**
- Deutsche Borse's $200M Kraken stake: TradFi exchanges are buying equity in crypto exchanges, not just listing crypto products.
- Goldman's BTC Premium Income ETF: structured BTC yield products entering TradFi — competes with onchain BTC yield.
- ZKsync's Cari Network ($600B deposits from 5 US banks): if real, this is the largest institutional L2 deployment ever.
- Base's Frosty AI auditor: Coinbase betting that AI can commoditize smart contract security.

**What to Test:**
- DNS security posture for any frontend you operate (DNSSEC, monitoring, registrar 2FA).
- Tokenized asset composability — can your protocol accept RWA tokens as collateral?
- Tether wallet UX — understand the onboarding flow they've built.

**What to Avoid:**
- Assuming DeFi security = smart contract security. Frontend attacks (DNS, supply chain) are now the dominant vector.
- Ignoring the stablecoin yield regulatory debate — the outcome directly determines what products are legal.
- Over-reliance on manual audits as the only security layer (Base's Frosty signals the shift).

**Open Questions:**
- Is the ZKsync/Cari $600B bank deposit claim real and live, or vaporware?
- Will Tether's wallet cannibalize existing USDT wallet providers or grow the overall pie?
- How will Aave's governance friction (DL News: DAO accusing Stani of "grabbing power") affect protocol development velocity?
- What happens to CoW Swap's market share post-hack? DNS attacks erode trust even when contracts are safe.
